## Prompt

Create a two-panel illustration sprite sheet for a WIC journalism website. Use the attached original grocery sprite sheet ONLY as a strict style reference: slightly irregular dark ink contours, flat soft colors, tiny dot eyes, simple natural hand-drawn figures, minimal detail. Match the original mother-and-child drawing, not generic vector clipart. Landscape canvas with two equal square cells, left and right, with generous separation, no labels or text. LEFT cell: a seated parent in a soft blue top cradling and breastfeeding a small baby, with a friendly seated lactation counselor beside her, a simple supportive conversation gesture. Modest everyday educational scene, unmistakable baby-care support. RIGHT cell: the same style parent holding her toddler's hand, receiving a referral paper from a health worker beside a simple clinic doorway marked by a small medical cross. Both complete scenes centered in their respective halves, same scale, no cropping at edges. White background, no ground shadows, no gradients, no photorealism, no 3D, no polished corporate icon geometry. Preserve the warmth and quirky linework of the reference.
